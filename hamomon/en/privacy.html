HAMOMON Privacy Policy

This policy describes the pre-release HAMOMON product candidate whose implementation was reviewed on October 13, 2026, and this support website. The policy will be updated if data handling changes.

Screen data and its purpose

With macOS screen recording permission, HAMOMON captures the target display using ScreenCaptureKit and renders visual effects locally on your Mac using Metal. Capture covers the entire display and may include content displayed by other apps. HAMOMON’s own display is excluded from capture.

Screen frames are processed as in-memory buffers and rendering textures. The reviewed implementation contains no code that saves screen frames as image or video files, transmits them over a network, or performs text recognition on screen content. On normal exit, capture stops and references to retained rendering textures are released. This does not guarantee immediate overwriting of memory.

System audio capture is disabled, and there is no microphone recording code. Click Sound plays audio bundled with the app.

Permission and stopping capture

You can manage or revoke screen recording permission in System Settings. Pause stops visual effects and click sounds, but does not stop screen capture. Capture also continues when effects are hidden because an app other than Finder is frontmost. To stop screen capture, choose “Quit HAMOMON” from the droplet menu.

Interaction information and local settings

To determine effect placement and visibility, HAMOMON reads pointer coordinates, left clicks, and identifiers of the frontmost app and the app owning the window at the click location locally on your Mac. It registers a specific pause shortcut (⌃⇧⌘P); there is no code that records general typed text.

Settings such as Interaction, Water Size, Movement, Click Sound, and pause state, along with whether screen recording access has been requested, are saved locally in UserDefaults. They are used on subsequent launches and for related app behavior.

Diagnostic logs

The current implementation writes diagnostics to macOS logging and outputs such as standard error. These may include click coordinates and timing, frontmost / click-target app identifiers, selected effects and sounds, display dimensions, capture status, performance measurements, and errors. There is no code that writes screen frames themselves to logs.

Log storage and retention depend on macOS and how the app is launched and logs are captured. HAMOMON does not set a fixed retention period. The reviewed implementation contains no automatic log upload code.

Communication and third parties

The reviewed app implementation contains no network communication, advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, tracking, or cloud synchronization code. It contains no code that automatically sends screen data, interaction information, settings, or diagnostic logs to the developer. This describes the app’s own behavior, rather than information separately handled by macOS or the App Store.

Information you send to support

If you contact support by email, the email address, message, and attachments you send reach the support inbox through email services. They are not sent automatically by the app. Review screenshots and logs, and send only the necessary portions.

Contact the address below for questions about storage, deletion, or handling of support correspondence. This policy does not assert unverified email service settings or retention periods.
