HAMOMON Support

HAMOMON is a Mac menu bar app that adds refraction responses to clicks in Finder windows and on the Finder desktop. Normal Finder operations continue to work. This guide covers the version 1.0 distribution candidate. Publishing this guide does not mean the app is on sale.

Getting started

  1. Launch HAMOMON. A droplet icon appears in the menu bar.
  2. Allow screen recording access in macOS settings when requested. If macOS asks you to quit and reopen, quit normally through the droplet menu and relaunch.
  3. Bring Finder to the front, then click inside a window or on the desktop.

With no saved preferences, defaults are WATER / Standard / Movement Off / Click Sound Off / not paused. Existing preferences, including Movement On and selected sounds, are restored. Audio in the demo illustrates explicitly enabled sounds.

Screen recording permission

Use “Open Screen Recording Settings…” in the droplet menu, or open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording (called “Screen & System Audio Recording” on some macOS versions), and allow HAMOMON.

This permission is needed to use the displayed screen to draw refraction effects. It is not a video-saving feature. Capture covers the target display, rather than only a small area beneath the pointer, and may include content displayed by other apps. System audio and microphone audio are not recorded.

Pausing or switching to another app does not stop screen capture. Choose “Quit HAMOMON” to stop capture. You can revoke permission in System Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How it works in Finder

Effects appear when Finder is the frontmost app. They are hidden when another app, such as Safari, is frontmost. If the first click used to switch to Finder does not produce an effect, click again after Finder is frontmost. HAMOMON does not edit Finder files or folders.

Using the droplet menu

  • Interaction: WATER (ripples), JELLY (elastic membrane), GRAVITY (inward pull), GLASS (hard refraction), BUBBLE (local lift), and SWEEP (a passing response).
  • Water Size: Standard / Wide, available only for WATER.
  • Movement: Off / On.
  • Click Sound: Off, Water C, Water D, Bubble A Short, CLACK, SWISH, Crystal, Glass A, and Glass B Short. Choose from six effects and eight click sounds independently, or leave sound Off. Sound is initially Off. Changing Interaction does not change your sound selection.
  • Pause / Resume: Use the menu or Control + Shift + Command + P (⌃⇧⌘P). This stops visual effects and click sounds; screen capture continues.

Quitting

Click the droplet icon in the menu bar and choose “Quit HAMOMON.” This ends screen capture, rendering, click sounds, and event monitoring.

Troubleshooting

  • No effects: Check screen recording permission, that Finder is frontmost and that HAMOMON is not paused.
  • No sound: Select a sound in Click Sound, check Pause, and check the Mac’s volume and output device.
  • No response after sleep: Capture retry after display wake has been improved and recovery verified in a limited physical test. Recovery is not guaranteed on every device or after every long sleep. If it fails, note the circumstances and try normal Quit. If quitting does not complete, avoid repeated actions and contact support.
  • Bright areas or fragments during window transitions: Opening/closing windows or rapid movement may leave temporary text/boundary remnants or bright fragments. A limited JELLY condition reduced closing white areas; artifacts are not eliminated for every effect or condition. Pause or quit normally if distracting.
  • Slow operation / shortcut conflict: Pause or quit from the droplet menu.

Include macOS/app versions, display setup, the action and time. Images and logs are optional. Send only necessary portions after removing private information. Do not send passwords, credentials, raw journals or raw boot-relative timestamps.
