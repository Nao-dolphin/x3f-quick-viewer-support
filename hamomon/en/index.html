HAMOMON Support

HAMOMON adds ripples and refraction effects to clicks and pointer movement in Finder and on the desktop. You can continue using Finder normally. This guide describes the product candidate being prepared for release.

Getting started

  1. Launch HAMOMON. A droplet icon appears in the menu bar instead of a regular main window.
  2. When asked for screen recording access, allow HAMOMON in macOS settings. Quit and relaunch HAMOMON after changing the permission.
  3. Click a Finder window or the desktop to bring Finder to the front. Try left clicks and pointer movement to see the effects.

Initial settings are WATER / Standard / Movement On / Click Sound Off / not paused. Saved settings are restored on subsequent launches.

Screen recording permission

Use “Open Screen Recording Settings…” in the droplet menu, or open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording (called “Screen & System Audio Recording” on some macOS versions), and allow HAMOMON.

This permission is needed to use the displayed screen to draw refraction effects. It is not a video-saving feature. Capture covers the target display, rather than only a small area beneath the pointer, and may include content displayed by other apps. System audio and microphone audio are not recorded.

Pausing or switching to another app does not stop screen capture. Choose “Quit HAMOMON” to stop capture. You can revoke permission in System Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How it works in Finder

Effects appear when Finder is the frontmost app. They are hidden when another app, such as Safari, is frontmost. If the first click used to switch to Finder does not produce an effect, click again after Finder is frontmost. HAMOMON does not edit Finder files or folders.

Using the droplet menu

  • Interaction: WATER (ripples), JELLY (elastic membrane), GRAVITY (inward pull), GLASS (hard refraction), BUBBLE (local lift), and SWEEP (a passing response).
  • Water Size: Standard / Wide, available only for WATER.
  • Movement: Off / On.
  • Click Sound: Off, Water C, Water D, Bubble A Short, CLACK, SWISH, Crystal, Glass A, and Glass B Short. Sound is initially Off. Changing Interaction does not change your sound selection.
  • Pause / Resume: Use the menu or Control + Shift + Command + P (⌃⇧⌘P). This stops visual effects and click sounds; screen capture continues.

Quitting

Click the droplet icon in the menu bar and choose “Quit HAMOMON.” This ends screen capture, rendering, click sounds, and event monitoring.

Troubleshooting

  • No effects / disabled controls: Check screen recording permission, that Finder is frontmost, and that HAMOMON is not paused. Quit and relaunch after granting permission.
  • No sound: Check that Click Sound is not Off, HAMOMON is not paused, and your Mac’s volume and output device are correct. Try a left click with Finder frontmost.
  • No response after sleep: The current candidate has a capture recovery issue after sleep; automatic recovery has not been verified. Try quitting and relaunching. This has not been verified as a guaranteed fix.
  • A white circle or unexpected image when windows hide: This is a known display issue during screen transitions. Its cause and a reliable workaround are not established. Pause or quit if it is distracting.
  • Slow operation / shortcut does not work: Pause or quit from the droplet menu. If the shortcut conflicts with another feature, use the menu.

If the issue persists, include your macOS version, HAMOMON version, display setup, and steps to reproduce it. Before sending screenshots or logs, check for personal information and information about other apps, and send only the necessary portions.
