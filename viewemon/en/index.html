VIEWEMON

Support

VIEWEMON helps you find photos across disks and folders you select on your Mac. It does not move, edit, or delete original files. AI-based categories and visual-similarity search run locally on your Mac.

Get started

  1. Use “Choose disks” or “Choose folders…” to select search locations, then grant access in the macOS picker.
  2. Wait for the first index to finish; this may take time for large libraries.
  3. Find photos with search, categories, albums, or “Find similar photos.”

Troubleshooting

  • For missing photos, clear search and filters, check Hidden in VIEWEMON, reconnect the original disk, and choose Sync now.
  • If access is denied, select the same disk or folder again and grant access in the macOS picker. Protected locations may remain inaccessible.
  • RAW and document files without a usable embedded preview may not be displayed.

Keep separate backups of important photos. Before sending photos or index files to support, check that they do not contain personal information.
