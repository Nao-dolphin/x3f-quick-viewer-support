Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Local processing

Photo analysis takes place entirely on your Mac. VIEWEMON does not send photos, thumbnails, AI features, or search terms to the developer or an external AI service. There are no accounts, ads, tracking, or usage analytics.

Data the app reads and stores

From disks and folders you authorize, the app reads photos and file information including names, locations, sizes, and modification dates. It stores an index, thumbnails, AI features, categories, albums, hidden-photo settings, and access bookmarks in local app storage. Treat this data as personal information, just like the original photos.

Retention and deletion

Removing a search location does not erase all cached or indexed data. Removing the app itself may also leave local data behind. To erase local library data, quit the app, use Finder’s Go → Go to Folder, and remove only VIEWEMON’s data at the following location. Do not delete your original photo folders.

~/Library/Containers/jp.nao.PhotoFinder.PoC/Data/Library/Application Support/Photo Finder/

Appearance preferences are stored under Preferences in the same app container. Index data and thumbnails may remain until you erase them; people sharing your Mac user account or able to access its backups may be able to view them.

Diagnostics, external apps, and contact

Performance diagnostic logging is disabled during normal launches. If developer diagnostics are explicitly enabled, timing and processing-count logs stay local and are not automatically transmitted. macOS backup and diagnostic features, and external apps you choose, follow their own settings and terms. Choosing “Open with” passes the original file URL through macOS to the application you select. VIEWEMON does not modify the original. Email and attachments you voluntarily send for support are used to respond to your request.
