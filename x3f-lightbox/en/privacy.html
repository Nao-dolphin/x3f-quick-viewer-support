Privacy Policy

Previews, filenames, photo metadata, decisions, rotation and app-generated device identifiers are exchanged directly between your selected Mac and iPad on the local network. No developer-operated server receives your photographs or review information. Original RAW files remain on the Mac. Saved sets remain on iPad for offline use; inactive sets can be deleted in the folder picker. Deleting the app removes its local data; synchronized decisions remain on the Mac. Pairing keys stay in the device Keychain and a Mac can be forgotten in the picker. The app includes no advertising, tracking, analytics or developer collection service. Apple may handle downloads, diagnostics and device backups under its policies. Email and attachments voluntarily sent to support are used to answer the request and retained only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations.

Requires iPadOS 17 or later. Japanese and English.
