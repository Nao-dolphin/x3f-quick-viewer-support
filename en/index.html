X3F Quick Viewer

A macOS utility for SIGMA X3F thumbnails and Quick Look previews

日本語EnglishPrivacy Policy

Getting started

  1. Launch X3F Quick Viewer once.
  2. Open a folder containing X3F files in Finder to see photo thumbnails.
  3. Select an X3F file and press the Space bar to open Quick Look.

The host app does not need to remain open.

If previews do not appear

  • Open the troubleshooting section in the app and check the macOS Quick Look extension settings.
  • Reopen Finder or restart the Mac to refresh the cache.
  • For speed testing, copy the X3F file to the Mac’s internal SSD.

Compatibility

Verified with representative files from dp1 Quattro, DP2 Merrill, SD1 Merrill, SD15, and an earlier-generation DP2.

Not every camera or shooting mode is guaranteed. Files without a valid embedded JPEG preview cannot be displayed.

Privacy

No photos or metadata are uploaded. All processing stays on the Mac and the original X3F is never modified.
